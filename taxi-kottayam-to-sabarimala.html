  • Kottayam Railway StationYour driver meets you at the station exit.

  • NilakkalBase camp for Sabarimala. The usual drop point in peak season.
  • PampaWhere the climb to Sannidhanam begins, when vehicles are allowed.

Nilakkal or Pampa: where will the cab stop?

In peak season private vehicles are usually stopped at Nilakkal, about 25 km before Pampa, and pilgrims continue by KSRTC bus. Rules change by date and crowd.

Sabarimala taxi questions

How far is Sabarimala from Kottayam?

About 95–100 km via Ponkunnam and Erumely. It takes 2.5–3.5 hours on normal days and longer during Mandalam.

Can the taxi go all the way to Pampa?

In peak season private vehicles are usually stopped at Nilakkal, about 25 km before Pampa. We confirm the drop point for your date.

How do I book from Kottayam Railway Station?

Send your train number, arrival time and number of pilgrims on WhatsApp. We confirm the cab and the driver meets you at the station exit.

When does Mandalam start in 2026?

The season is scheduled from 17 November 2026, with Mandala Pooja on 27 December and Makaravilakku on 14 January 2027. Confirm final dates with the Travancore Devaswom Board.

Can I book from another state?

Yes. Message us before boarding your train and the driver waits at the station exit. We reply in Malayalam, English, Tamil and Hindi.

Do you offer return trips?

Yes. The driver can wait, or you can book a pickup from Nilakkal or Pampa for your return time.
