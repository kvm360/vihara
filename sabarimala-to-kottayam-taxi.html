Sabarimala return taxi questions

Is there a taxi from Sabarimala to Kottayam?

Yes. My Vihara Cabs runs 24/7 return taxis from Pampa and Nilakkal to Kottayam and Kottayam Railway Station. Pre-book on WhatsApp so a cab is ready when you come down.

Can the taxi pick me up at Pampa?

In peak season private vehicles are usually stopped at Nilakkal, about 25 km before Pampa, so pickup is normally at Nilakkal. We confirm the pickup point for your date.

Can the driver wait while I go for darshan?

Yes. The driver can wait at Nilakkal for the whole trip, or you can book a separate pickup for your return time.

How long does it take from Sabarimala to Kottayam?

About 2.5 to 3.5 hours for the 95 to 100 km drive on normal days. Allow 5 to 6 hours on Mandalam peak days.

How do I make sure I catch my train from Kottayam?

Share your train number when you book and keep a generous buffer after darshan. We plan the pickup around your train and drop you at the station.

How far is Nilakkal from Pampa?

About 25 km. In peak season pilgrims travel between the two by KSRTC bus.
