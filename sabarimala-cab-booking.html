Sabarimala cab booking questions

Can I book a cab to Sabarimala online?

Yes. Choose your pickup, drop and vehicle on this page and send the request on WhatsApp. We reply to confirm your cab and quote.

Do I need to download an app to book?

No. The booking page works in your phone browser and the request reaches us on WhatsApp.

Can I book a cab from Sabarimala back to Kottayam?

Yes. Switch to From Sabarimala and choose Nilakkal or Pampa as your pickup. We confirm the pickup point for your date.

Which pickup and drop points do you cover?

Kottayam Railway Station, Kottayam town, Erumely, Nilakkal and Pampa where vehicles are allowed.

Can the cab go all the way to Pampa?

In peak season private vehicles are usually stopped at Nilakkal, about 25 km before Pampa, and pilgrims continue by KSRTC bus. We confirm the drop point for your date.

How do I pay?

Pay after the ride by UPI or cash.
