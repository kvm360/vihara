Sabarimala by train: find your state

Special trains for the season usually run from Chennai, Bangalore, Hyderabad and Vijayawada, and the Railways announces the timetable each year. Whichever train you take, we meet you at Kottayam.

How to pre-book before you board

  1. Message us your train number, arrival time and group size.
  2. We confirm your vehicle, drop point and quote on WhatsApp.
  3. Share delays. If your train runs late, message us the new time.
  4. Meet your driver at the Kottayam Railway Station exit.
  5. Pay after the ride by UPI or cash.

What to expect after you arrive

The road trip to Pampa takes 2.5–3.5 hours on normal days and 5–6 hours on peak Mandalam days. Cabs have room for irumudi and bags.

In peak season private vehicles are usually stopped at Nilakkal, about 25 km before Pampa, and pilgrims continue by KSRTC bus. We confirm your drop point before you leave.

Travelling in a group of 10 or more? Ask for a Tempo Traveller, or several vehicles with one contact number.

Questions from out-of-state pilgrims

How do I reach Sabarimala from Chennai, Bangalore or Hyderabad?

Most pilgrims take a train to Kottayam or Chengannur and continue by road. Kerala Tourism lists Kottayam as the most convenient station for pilgrims coming from outside Kerala. From Kottayam Railway Station the drive to Pampa is about 95 to 100 km.

Can I pre-book a cab before my train reaches Kottayam?

Yes. Send your train number, arrival time and group size on WhatsApp before you board. We confirm the cab and your driver waits at the station exit.

Which languages do you reply in?

We reply in Malayalam, English, Tamil and Hindi. You can start a message in Telugu or Kannada and we will help in English or Hindi.

Will the cab drop me at Nilakkal or Pampa?

In peak season private vehicles are usually stopped at Nilakkal, about 25 km before Pampa, and pilgrims continue by KSRTC bus. We confirm the drop point for your date.

Can a large group book one vehicle?

Yes. Tell us your group size and we arrange a Tempo Traveller or more than one vehicle, with one contact number for the whole group.

Can you pick us up for the train home?

Yes. Share your return train number and we plan the pickup at Nilakkal or Pampa with that train in mind. Allow a generous buffer after darshan.

When does the Mandalam season start in 2026?

The season is scheduled from 17 November 2026, with Mandala Pooja on 27 December and Makaravilakku on 14 January 2027. Check the Railways announcement for this year's special trains.
