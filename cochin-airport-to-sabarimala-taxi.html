Cochin airport to Sabarimala taxi

My Vihara Cabs runs 24/7 taxis from Cochin International Airport (Kochi) to Erumely, Nilakkal and Pampa. The drive is about 110–120 km and takes around 3.5–4 hours on normal days. Send your flight number on WhatsApp and your driver meets you at arrivals.

Flying in? Book before you land

  1. Message us your flight number, arrival time and group size.
  2. We confirm your vehicle, drop point and quote on WhatsApp.
  3. Share delays. If your flight is late, message us the new time.
  4. Meet your driver at the arrival exit and load your irumudi and bags.
  5. Pay after the ride by UPI or cash.

Nilakkal or Pampa?

In peak season private vehicles are usually stopped at Nilakkal, about 25 km before Pampa, and pilgrims continue by KSRTC bus. Rules change by date and crowd.

Tell us your travel date and we confirm the drop point before you land.

Landing late at night? Tell us if you want to start straight away or rest first, and we plan the drive around you.

Which route does the driver take?

There are three usual ways from the airport to Pampa. Your driver picks the best one for the day’s traffic and your drop point.

Main Central Road

Via Thiruvalla and Ranni towards Pampa.

Pala and Kanjirappally

Via Perumbavoor, Muvattupuzha, Koothattukulam, Pala and Kanjirappally.

Pampady road

Via Pampady and Pangadapally towards Pampa.

Cochin airport to Sabarimala questions

How far is Cochin airport from Sabarimala?

About 110 to 120 km by road to Pampa, depending on the route. The drive takes around 3.5 to 4 hours on normal days and longer during Mandalam peak days.

Can I pre-book a taxi before my flight lands?

Yes. Send your flight number, arrival time and group size on WhatsApp. We confirm the cab and your driver meets you at the arrival exit. If your flight is delayed, message us the new time.

Which airport is nearest to Sabarimala for pilgrims?

Most pilgrims fly into Cochin International Airport at Nedumbassery. Thiruvananthapuram airport is a longer drive, around 200 km.

Will the taxi go to Nilakkal or Pampa?

In peak season private vehicles are usually stopped at Nilakkal, about 25 km before Pampa, and pilgrims continue by KSRTC bus. We confirm the drop point for your date.

I land late at night. Can I still book?

Yes. We run 24 hours. Tell us if you want to start straight away or rest first, and we plan the drive around you.

Can you take me back to the airport after darshan?

Yes. Book a pickup at Nilakkal or Pampa and share your flight time. Leave a generous buffer for the 3.5 to 4 hour drive, peak-day traffic and airport check-in.

Do I pay in advance?

No. Pay after the ride by UPI or cash.
